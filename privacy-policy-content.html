1. Introduction

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time and for any reason. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this Privacy Policy.

  • Name: Your full name for account creation and communication

  • SMS Opt-In: When you voluntarily provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages through our contact forms, verbal consent, or written forms

5. SMS Consent and Mobile Opt-In

Important Information About Text Messaging:

5.1 Voluntary Participation

Providing your phone number and consenting to receive SMS messages is completely voluntary. You are not required to opt in to SMS communications to use our services or make purchases.

5.2 Mobile Opt-In

By providing your phone number and checking the SMS consent box on our contact forms, or by providing verbal or written consent, you expressly consent to receive text messages from dub.

5.3 Types of SMS Messages

If you opt in, you may receive messages related to:

  • Order confirmations and updates
  • Account notifications
  • Shipping and delivery updates
  • Billing inquiries
  • Promotions or offers (if applicable)

5.4 Phone Number Privacy

Phone numbers obtained as part of the SMS consent process will NOT be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Your phone number will only be used by dub to communicate with you directly.

5.5 Message Frequency

Message frequency may vary depending on your orders and interactions with us. You may receive up to 4 SMS messages per week related to your orders, account, or activity.

Example Message:

"Hey! Your dub order has shipped and is on its way. Track it here: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messaging from dub at any time."

5.6 Potential Fees

Please note that standard message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier's pricing plan. These fees may vary if the message is sent domestically or internationally.

5.7 Opt-Out Methods

You can opt out at any time by:

  • Replying "STOP" to any text message
  • Emailing us at support@getdub.com
  • Contacting us through our contact form

5.8 Help

If you are experiencing any issues, you can reply with the keyword HELP. Or, you can get help directly from us at support@getdub.com or visit our contact page.

5.9 Additional Options

If you do not wish to receive SMS messages, you can choose not to check the SMS consent box on our forms or decline when asked for consent verbally or in writing.
